MUATIM — ISP billing & payments platform

Internet providers run on three things: customers, bandwidth and money. MUATIM is the system I designed and built to run all three — a complete billing platform for ISPs where M-Pesa is a first-class citizen, not an afterthought bolted onto a voucher system.

THE PROBLEM

ISP operators were juggling spreadsheets, WhatsApp payment screenshots and manual router configs. Reconciliation was manual, renewals were missed, and growth meant more chaos.

THE APPROACH

One dependable platform: customers and packages in a clean billing engine, M-Pesa STK Push payments verified by callback, everything reconciled automatically, and network operations wired in.

  • Payments that reconcile themselves — Daraja API integration: STK Push initiated in-app, callback verified, balance updated, receipt logged. Failure paths handled and retried.
  • Billing engine — packages, billing cycles, auto-renewal via customer balance, expiry handling and payment history.
  • Network operations — MikroTik router management, hotspot & PPPoE flows, voucher generation and bandwidth management.
  • Operational surface — admin dashboards, customer-facing self-service screens, SMS/notification hooks and scheduled cron automation.
